Privacy Policy for Qux Kittu

Last Updated: September 21, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Qux Kittu ("the Bot") collects, how it is used, and how you can request its deletion.

1. Information We Collect

To provide its core features and commands, the Bot may automatically collect and store the following basic Discord data:

  • User IDs: To identify users, save user-specific settings, or manage permissions.
  • Server (Guild) IDs: To store server-specific configurations (such as custom prefixes, welcome channels, or moderation logs).
  • Channel & Role IDs: To execute commands in designated channels or assign roles properly.
  • Command Usage Data: Temporary logs of commands executed to help diagnose errors and prevent spam.

Note: The Bot does NOT collect or store your personal messages, passwords, IP addresses, or payment information.

2. How We Use Information

The collected data is used strictly to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Bot's functionality.
  • Remember server and user preferences.
  • Prevent abuse, spam, and unauthorized access.

3. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect stored data from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. Data is stored securely and is only accessible by the Bot's core developer(s).

4. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell, trade, rent, or share your data with any third parties. Data will only be disclosed if required by law or to comply with Discord's Developer Policy.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your data only for as long as the Bot remains in your server or as needed to provide its services.

  • Automatic Deletion: When the Bot is removed (kicked/banned) from a server, server-specific configurations are automatically deleted or scheduled for removal within 30 days.
  • Manual Request: You have the right to request the immediate deletion of all data associated with your User ID or Server ID at any time by contacting us.

6. Contact & Data Requests

To request data access, data deletion, or to ask questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via Discord: @abcxwy or join our support server: https://discord.gg.
